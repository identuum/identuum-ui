**Failure decomposition — no failure is a UI-behaviour bug; every one is harness/state
coupling:** (a) authenticated specs log in with env-file credentials that only exist on
the owner's long-lived seeded stack — against a FRESH released backend they time out in
`beforeAll` (~180 s each; those timeouts are what stretched the run to 38 minutes);
(b) the tail cluster (site-admin-settings, upgrade-backup, verify-email) failed on
`ERR_CONNECTION_REFUSED` — the `next dev` webServer died partway through the run;
(c) the org-admin family self-skips because dynamic-fixture mode is off — and could
not be turned on: its global-setup preflight probes `/app/identuum` and an `/e2e-auth`
bind mount inside the IDP container, which is the PRE-SPLIT MONOLITH contract; the
released distroless appliance has `/app/identuum-idp` and no such mount.

## STALE-PIN: the UI's own contract spec asserts the scaffold era

`oss-contract.spec.ts` passed 5/5 against v0.3.0 — and two of those passes are
vacuous-by-shape (V2): the header still reads *"No auth, no /authorize, no /token"* —
verbatim the "old scaffold lie" that identuum-idp-oss's own
`TestOSSContract_NoScaffoldFraming` bans from its main.go — and the negative pins
(`GET /authorize MUST NOT be 200/302`, `POST /token MUST NOT be 200`) go green
against a backend that SERVES both endpoints, because a parameterless probe gets a
4xx. The spec certifies the absence of the product's core feature and passes while
the feature ships.
